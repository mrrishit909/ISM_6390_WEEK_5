Airline Digital Maturity Assessment

A 20-question diagnostic calibrated to passenger airlines. It scores five capability dimensions from 1 to 5, plots them on a radar chart, and reads the pattern of your scores as a strategic profile.

5 dimensions20 questionsAbout 10 minutesNothing is stored or sent

How to answer

Answer for the airline as a whole. For each question, choose the highest option that is fully true today, not what is planned or piloted at one station. Every question must be answered before results appear.
