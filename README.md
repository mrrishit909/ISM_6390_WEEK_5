# Airline Digital Maturity Assessment

**ISM 6390 · Digital Business Transformation Foundations · Week 5 Group Activity**

A single-file, browser-only diagnostic that scores a passenger airline on five airline-specific capability dimensions. It draws a Chart.js radar chart and interprets the score *pattern* as one of five strategic profiles.

**Run it:** open `index.html` in Chrome or Firefox. It needs no server, stores nothing, and loads Chart.js from the jsDelivr CDN.
